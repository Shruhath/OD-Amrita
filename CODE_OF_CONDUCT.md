# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's
**Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), marking the report as a conduct issue, or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove contributions and block participants who break this code.
